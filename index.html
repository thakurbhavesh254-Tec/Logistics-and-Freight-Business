Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about weight limits, routes, or customs regulations? Let us guide you.

How does your AI routing optimize shipment delivery? +

Our routing engines analyze traffic density data from national highway APIs, weather forecasts, and historical delay indexes. When blockages occur, our system dynamically generates alternative directions and alerts drivers via the dispatcher portal.

Which cities do you support for express air freight? +

Currently, express air delivery supports airport-to-airport routes connecting Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Pune. We offer last-mile hub transport for adjacent locations within 100km.

Is cargo insurance included in bookings? +

All standard bookings include default transit liability coverage up to ₹10,000. For higher value commercial assets, you can select premium comprehensive protection during step 2 of our booking portal, covering damages up to ₹5,00,000.
